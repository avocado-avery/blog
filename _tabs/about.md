I'm **Avery Hughes**, a security engineer and analyst finishing a B.S. in Cybersecurity at Indiana Institute of Technology (May 2027). I like building the infrastructure, then figuring out how to break it, then writing down how to stop that from happening.

This blog is where I put malware writeups, homelab builds, and notes from things I'm working on.


## What I do

**Security Analyst, Indiana Cyber Network**
Security assessments for clients across the defense industrial base, manufacturing, local government, education, and nonprofit sectors. I investigate findings, tailor testing to compliance needs like NIST SP 800-53 and FedRAMP, work with client IT teams on fixes, and present results to leadership.

**Security Engineering, DMA**
Building a malware analysis lab (CAPEv2, FLARE VM, REMnux), managing CrowdStrike Falcon EDR and automating it with FalconPy and Ansible, and running vulnerability management pipelines out of GitLab's security tooling.

## Projects

- **[ClassCloud](https://classcloud.net)**: a Python/Flask web platform with SSO, published through Cloudflare Tunnel
- **Homelab**: Proxmox cluster with an OPNsense router, segmented VLANs, HAProxy with wildcard TLS, and NetBird for remote access. Most of what I write about gets tested here first
- **Senior project**: an autonomous malware analysis lab in Azure that detonates samples in throwaway VMs and uses an LLM to draft the analysis report
